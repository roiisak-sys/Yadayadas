# STARDUST rebrand – announcement posts

Facts used (all from the site): six Israeli musicians · four years on stage · violin, sax and percussion
on top of live rock'n'roll · special arrangements · "a tribute, not an imitation" · the music told together
with Bowie's life story and the way he read himself and the world.
New name: **STARDUST – David Bowie Tribute** (formerly YADAYADAS). Site: https://stardustbowie.com
(English) · https://stardustbowie.com/he/ (Hebrew). Instagram @stardustbowie.il ·
Facebook "Stardust - David Bowie Tribute" (facebook.com/stardustbowie.il).
Next show: London Festival | The Green Bear, Hod HaSharon, 7.11.26 (check the event page before posting).

Suggested posting order: Facebook + Instagram on day 1 (post 1), LinkedIn the same day, the "why the
new name" post two or three days later, the show post the week of the show. Post from the Page /
account that was renamed, so existing followers get it. Pin post 1 on both pages.

Visual for post 1: `profile-logo-1080.png` on a black square, or a screenshot of the new hero.
Visual for the show post: the London Festival poster.

## Post 2 – why the new name (Facebook / Instagram, 2–3 days later)

### Hebrew
```
למה סטארדאסט? ⭐

כי "Stardust" הוא יותר משם של שיר. זה האבק שבואי השאיר מאחוריו בכל דמות שיצר ועזב –
זיגי, הדוכס הלבן, התקופה הברלינאית – ומה שאנחנו מנסים לשחזר על הבמה: לא את המראה, אלא את המסע.

אז שינינו את השם, והשארנו את מה שחשוב: מוזיקה חיה, עיבודים מיוחדים, והסיפור בין השירים.

מחכים לראות אתכם בהופעה הבאה. הפרטים באתר 👇
stardustbowie.com/he/
```

### English
```
Why Stardust? ⭐

Because Stardust is more than a song title. It's what Bowie left behind in every character he
created and walked away from – Ziggy, the Thin White Duke, the Berlin years – and what we try to
bring to the stage: not the look, but the journey.

So we changed the name and kept what matters: live music, special arrangements, and the story
between the songs.

See you at the next show. Details on the site 👇
stardustbowie.com
```

## LinkedIn – personal post (from Roi)

### English
```
Four years ago a few friends and I started playing David Bowie's songs together. This week the
band got a new name.

YADAYADAS is now STARDUST – David Bowie Tribute.

Why change? Because the old name said nothing about the thing that makes the show different.
We're six Israeli musicians who play live, kicking rock'n'roll with violin, sax and percussion.
But what people remember is what happens between the songs: Bowie's life, the psychology behind the
characters he built and abandoned, and how he read himself and the world.

It's a tribute, not an imitation. And a rebrand is a good moment to say that out loud.

What changed: the name, the logo, the website (stardustbowie.com), and the social pages.
What didn't: the band, the music, and the way we tell the story.

If you book shows or run a venue or a cultural hall, I'd be glad to talk.
stardustbowie.com

#Rebranding #LiveMusic #DavidBowie #Music #Entertainment
```
